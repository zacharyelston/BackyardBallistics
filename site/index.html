SpudGunGolf

A backyard trajectory game for a low-power launcher. Each hole is a guyed pole: range it from photos, fire, spot each round from the firing point, let fire control correct, and score rounds fired plus metres outside the ring.

Fire, spot each round, adjust by the mean error, cease fire and verify. Four fixed-firing-point holes and five downrange-play holes. Par 56.

The game is a simulation. Real play is low power only: inanimate targets on your own property, within the limits in SafetyKit (45 m/s, 100 yd, a clear ±30° fan).
